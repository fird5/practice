Rules of the Plan

  • Eligibility: Only full-time staff who have successfully completed their probation period are eligible for the QPB. Staff must be actively employed for the full quarter to qualify.
  • Transparency: Surplus figures are calculated and verified by the Mosque Management Board (MMB) after each quarter.
  • Leading Performance Discussions: Performance weights are assigned before the quarter ends. Adjustments are discussed proactively between the KAH and the staff member.
  • MMB Governance: All weight adjustments are subject to MMB review. The Board reserves the right to adjust calculations based on extraordinary financial events.
